## [v0.1.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- First public release: Pawgger, a Frogger parody (Colby the cat, Burger King to Micro Center).
- Three switchable styles: retro 8-bit, ghibli painterly, toon (character library).
- Generated, validated levels with day, dusk and night cycles; home-bay bonuses and lane pickups.
- Autopilot bot clearing levels 1-10 on seeds 1-10.
- Single-file offline build (`node tools/build-web.mjs`), GitHub Pages deployment, Apache-2.0 licence.
